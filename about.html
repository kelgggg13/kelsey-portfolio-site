Hi, it's me. I'm Kelsey.

I'm a Senior Product Designer with 6 years of experience across early-stage and growth-stage startups. I'm currently at Cohere Health, where I design tools that help healthcare teams work faster and make better decisions.

I care most about design for social good, using research and craft to build things that make a real, measurable difference in people's lives.

Whether that's health tech, access, or something else entirely, I believe good design starts with evidence, not assumptions, and that the best solutions come from getting close to the people you're designing for.

Outside of work, I'm usually watching comedies, traveling, or developing recipes. I document most of that (mainly the food) on my food Instagram.
