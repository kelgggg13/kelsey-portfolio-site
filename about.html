How I use AI

I use it as a tool to assist my process, not to replace it

AI helps me test out edge cases, synthesize research faster, and refine writing. But I set the direction, because saving time doesn’t matter if I’m moving toward the wrong goal.

If it feels “AI generated”, it isn’t done

I am responsible for the words and design I put out. Saying “Claude generated this so there may be mistakes” is not good enough.

AI allows me to do more, not to cut corners

The time I used to spend wiring up prototypes in Figma now goes back into the work that matters most: framing the problem, talking to users, and building alignment across teams.
